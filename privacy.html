Privacy Policy

Yoni's Stream · Version 1.1 · Effective November 14, 2026

In short: Yoni's Stream is a live streaming and recording studio that runs on your phone. It has no servers of its own, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not sell data. Your image and voice only leave the phone toward the platforms you set up (Twitch, YouTube, Kick, TikTok…). Your scenes, keys and settings are stored on your phone and, if you choose, in your own Google Drive.

1. Who is responsible

Yoni's Stream is an app created by @yonyjrtolentino ("we"). For any privacy question, write to yonisstreamstudio@gmail.com.

2. What data the app uses and why

  • Camera, microphone, internal audio and screen. Used only when you turn them on, to compose your stream or recording on the phone. Screen and internal audio capture need your permission every time (Android asks). Video and audio are sent only to the destinations you configure (for example Twitch or YouTube) or saved to your gallery. We never receive them.
  • Stream keys, server addresses, channel names and settings. Stored in the app's private storage on your phone and used to connect to your platforms. They only leave the phone inside your backups (see section 4).
  • Google account (optional). If you sign in with Google, the app receives your name, email and profile photo to show which account is linked, plus permission to store files in the app's private folder in your Google Drive ("appDataFolder"). The app cannot see any other file in your Drive. You can sign out at any time.
  • Stream chat. If you turn it on, the app reads the public chat messages of your channels (Twitch, YouTube, Kick, TikTok and Discord) to show them in your stream and, if you ask, to read them aloud with your phone's text-to-speech engine. They are not kept after the studio closes.
  • Purchases. Subscriptions are paid through Google Play. We never see your card or payment details. Google Play tells the app on your phone which plan you have so its features can be unlocked.
  • Error reports. If the app closes because of an error, it saves a technical report on your phone (model, Android and app version, and where it failed; never your keys or personal data). It only reaches us if you choose to email it from "Report a problem".
  • Ratings and updates. In the Google Play version, the app may show Google's window to rate it (after a stream that went well) and tell you about new versions. Google Play handles this; we do not receive your in-app rating or any of your data. When a new version exists, the app also reads a public file on this website (actualizacion.json) to know whether the update is urgent; that request sends none of your data.
  • Local network. To receive video from a console or another phone, the app opens a server on your Wi-Fi and announces itself on your local network. It is only reachable from your network and uses a code to control the studio.

3. Who it is shared with

We do not share your data with third parties and we do not sell it. The app only connects, at your request, to:

  • The streaming platforms and servers you configure, to send your video and read your chat. Each applies its own privacy policy.
  • Google Play (subscriptions), Google (sign-in and Google Drive, if you use them) and Google Fonts (to download the fonts you pick).
  • Discord, only if you set up a go-live notice (webhook) or your server's chat.
  • The web pages you add as stream layers (alerts, widgets…).
  • The IP cameras and devices on your local network that you connect (RTSP or RTMP).
  • Cloudflare (speed.cloudflare.com), only if you measure your internet from the assistant: random test data is sent, nothing of yours.

4. Backups

  • Android backup: if Google backup is on for your phone, Android saves your scenes, overlays and settings in your Google account and restores them when you reinstall. Images and videos are not included.
  • Google Drive backup (optional): while signed in, the app stores a full copy (scenes, overlays, images, videos, sounds, fonts, destinations with their keys and settings) in the app's private folder in your Google Drive. Only you, through this app, can read it.
  • Backup file: you can save a .zip wherever you want. That file is not encrypted and includes your stream keys: keep it somewhere safe.

5. Phone permissions

Camera and microphone (to stream and record), notifications (stream status), display over other apps (the control bubble over your games), foreground service (so the stream keeps going while you play), USB devices (capture cards and Stream Deck, when you plug them in) and network and Wi-Fi state. The app does not ask for your contacts, calls, SMS, location or photos.

6. Retention and deletion

  • App data stays on your phone until you delete it. You can delete everything in Settings › Privacy and terms › Delete all my data, or by uninstalling the app.
  • Your Google Drive copy is deleted in Settings › Google account and backup › Delete the Google Drive backup, or from Google Drive › Settings › Manage apps.
  • To remove the app's access to your Google account: myaccount.google.com › Security › Third-party apps.
  • If you emailed us, we delete your emails when you ask.

7. Security

Your data is stored in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot access. Connections to Google and to platforms that support it (RTMPS) are encrypted. The app's code is obfuscated to make tampering harder.

8. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13. Each streaming platform has its own minimum age (for example 16 on YouTube and 18 on TikTok LIVE).

9. Your rights

Because we do not keep your data on our servers, you control it directly from the app. If you still want to exercise your rights of access, correction, deletion or objection under your local law (for example the GDPR or the CCPA), email yonisstreamstudio@gmail.com and we will answer within 30 days.

10. Changes

If we change this policy, the app will show you the new version and ask you to accept it before continuing.
