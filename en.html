Features

Everything your stream needs

Go live IRL on Twitch and YouTube with live overlays from the comfort of your phone. Record your videos with overlays like a pro.

01 / 06

A STUDIO IN YOUR POCKET

  • Scenes with layers: rear and front camera, your phone's screen and games, images, videos, text, browser and chat.
  • Studio mode with preview and transitions: cut, fade, slide, move and stinger.
  • Horizontal 16:9 and vertical 9:16 canvases at the same time, for TikTok, Instagram and YouTube Shorts.
  • Privacy shield: covers the stream during a call or when you leave your game.
02 / 06

LIVE OVERLAYS

  • Bars, captions, tickers, goals, countdowns and your socials, with 2000+ fonts.
  • Automatic alerts for subs, bits, raids, Super Chats, members, Kicks and TikTok gifts, with sounds and effects (confetti, hearts, fireworks…).
  • Chat commands so your viewers can trigger effects and sounds.
  • Twitch, YouTube, Kick, TikTok and Discord chat together on stream, with text-to-speech.
  • Export your overlays to OBS and Streamlabs, or import your own.
03 / 06

MULTISTREAM AND RECORDING

  • Stream to several platforms at once over RTMP, RTMPS or SRT: Twitch, YouTube, Kick, TikTok LIVE, Instagram Live, Facebook, X or your own server.
  • Record up to 4K at 60 fps with pause, and save clips of the last few seconds.
  • Hardware H.264 and HEVC, adaptive bitrate and automatic reconnection.
  • Heat and battery management for streams that last for hours.
0 dB−6−12−24−48
04 / 06

PRO AUDIO

  • Mixer with microphone, internal audio from games and apps, music and sound effects.
  • Voice/game balance, ducking, noise suppression, compressor and output limiter.
  • Ready-made profiles: game & voice, talk, podcast, background music, ASMR and IRL outdoors.
05 / 06

CONSOLES, CAPTURE CARDS AND CAMERAS

  • USB (UVC) capture cards, including Elgato.
  • PS5 and PS4 over Wi-Fi with no capture card, a PC with OBS, or another phone as a sender.
  • IP cameras over RTSP and action cameras over RTMP.
  • Elgato Stream Deck over USB and on-screen shortcut buttons.

PLANS

  • No plan: build scenes and overlays.
  • 5-day free trial (new accounts): streaming and recording up to 1080p at 30 fps, 2 h a day.
  • Deluxe Studio: up to 1080p at 60 fps, 5 h of streaming a day, multistream to 3 platforms and automatic reconnection.
  • Streamer: everything unlimited (4K60, unlimited scenes and overlays, all platforms, Stream Deck, capture cards and pro cameras, all fonts, audio profiles, filters, assistant and chat text-to-speech).

Subscriptions renew monthly and you can cancel anytime in Google Play.

PRIVACY

No ads, no tracking. Your video only goes to the platforms you choose; your scenes and keys stay on your phone and, if you want, in your own Google Drive.
