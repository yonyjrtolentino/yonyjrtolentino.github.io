Delete your Yoni's Stream data

Yoni's Stream has no servers of its own: we do not keep accounts or data about you. Everything is on your phone and, if you chose so, in your own Google Drive. Here is how to delete it:

1. Everything on the phone

In the app: Settings › Privacy and terms › Delete all my data. Scenes, overlays, keys, sounds, fonts, settings and the Google session are deleted. Uninstalling the app also deletes everything.

2. Your Google Drive backup

In the app: Settings › Google account and backup › Delete the Google Drive backup. Or without the app: open Google Drive › Settings › Manage apps, find Yoni's Stream › Options › Delete hidden app data.

3. Your Google account permission

In the app: Sign out. Or at myaccount.google.com › Security › Third-party apps, choose Yoni's Stream › Remove access.

4. Emails with support

If you wrote to us and want your messages deleted, ask at yonisstreamstudio@gmail.com. We do it within 30 days.

Subscriptions are managed in Google Play: canceling does not delete data and deleting data does not cancel the subscription (cancel it in Google Play › Payments & subscriptions).
